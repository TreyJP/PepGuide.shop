Weight loss 

Retatrutide-  Retatrutide is an investigational triple-agonist peptide that has delivered top-tier weight-loss results, major metabolic improvements, and promising liver-fat reduction in clinical trials, making it one of the most potent obesity drugs in development.
Cagrilintide — A long-acting amylin analog aimed at producing strong appetite suppression and meaningful weight loss, especially when paired with other anti-obesity agents.
Semaglutide — A GLP-1 peptide that reliably drives significant weight loss and better blood sugar control, with proven real-world results.
Tirzepatide — A dual-action peptide that produces some of the strongest weight-loss and metabolic improvements currently available.
Mazdutide — A GLP-1 and glucagon dual agonist designed to deliver substantial fat loss along with stronger metabolic effects.
Survodutide — A dual-agonist peptide that has shown impressive weight-loss potential and promising improvements in liver and metabolic health.
Orforglipron — A once-daily oral GLP-1 weight-loss drug that helps reduce appetite and body weight, with clinical trials showing meaningful fat loss.
Amycretin — A next-generation GLP-1 and amylin-based peptide showing very strong early results for rapid weight loss.
AOD-9604 — A fragment peptide marketed for fat loss, but with limited evidence for producing meaningful results.
Tesofensine* — A potent appetite- and craving-reducing compound that can drive weight loss, though tolerability can be a limiting factor.
5-Amino-1MQ* — A metabolic peptide-like compound focused on improving fat loss and energy use, with early-stage promise but limited human proof.
MOTS-c — A mitochondrial peptide that is studied for improving metabolism, energy, and exercise efficiency, with emerging but still limited evidence.
Tesamorelin — A growth-hormone-releasing peptide best known for reducing visceral fat and improving body composition, especially in specific medical settings.
SR9009 — A REV-ERB agonist often described as a fat-loss compound because it may increase energy expenditure and shift metabolism toward greater calorie burning.
SR9011 — A related REV-ERB agonist that is also discussed in fat-loss contexts for its potential to boost metabolic rate and improve energy use.
GW501516 — A PPAR-delta agonist that is often described as a endurance and fat-loss compound because it may improve fat oxidation and energy use.
GW0742 — A related PPAR-delta agonist that is also discussed in fat-loss and performance contexts for its potential to shift the body toward better fat burning and endurance.
Methylene blue — A mitochondrial-support compound that may help fat loss indirectly by improving energy production, exercise output, and dieting tolerance, but it is not a proven direct fat burner. 
Albuterol — A short-acting beta-2 agonist that can mildly support fat loss by increasing metabolic rate and lipolysis, but it is not a proven or recommended fat-loss drug. 
Clenbuterol — A beta-2 agonist that is often used for fat loss because it can increase metabolic rate and lipolysis, but it carries significant cardiovascular risk.
GC-1 — A TRβ-selective thyromimetic that can raise metabolism, support fat loss, and improve cholesterol while aiming to avoid the stronger heart-stimulating effects of full thyroid hormone.
NMN — A NAD+ precursor best known for supporting cellular energy and metabolism, with some early evidence for modest fat-loss and body-composition benefits.  
Tesofensine — A potent appetite-suppressing weight-loss compound that works by boosting norepinephrine, serotonin, and dopamine signaling, with strong clinical results but some heart-rate and stimulant-type side effects. 
BAM15 — A selective mitochondrial uncoupler that increases cellular energy expenditure by dissipating the proton gradient, shown in animal studies to raise oxygen consumption, increase fat oxidation, reverse diet‑induced obesity, and improve insulin sensitivity while sparing lean mass. 


Healing Peptides
BPC-157 — A healing-focused peptide with strong preclinical promise for repairing muscle, tendon, and gut tissue, but limited human proof.
TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment) — A regenerative peptide aimed at speeding soft-tissue recovery and repair, with mostly early-stage evidence.
GHK-Cu — A skin-repair and cosmetic peptide known for supporting collagen, wound healing, and overall skin quality.
KPV — An anti-inflammatory peptide studied mainly for gut and tissue calming, with emerging healing potential.
LL-37 — An antimicrobial peptide with wound-healing potential, but its effects are more specialized and less proven in humans.
ARA-290 (Cibinetide) — A tissue-protective peptide being studied for inflammation and nerve repair, with promising but limited clinical results.
Thymosin Alpha-1 (Tα1) — An immune-supportive peptide with real clinical use, especially for regulating immune response and recovery.
CJC-1295 — A growth-hormone–releasing peptide designed to raise GH and support recovery, body composition, and repair.
Ipamorelin — A gentler growth-hormone–releasing peptide used to promote recovery, sleep, and lean-tissue support.
PEG-MGF — A muscle-repair peptide aimed at stimulating localized regeneration and post-injury recovery.
IGF-1 LR3 — A powerful anabolic peptide used to support muscle growth and tissue repair, though with a stronger risk profile.
LL-37 — A host-defense peptide that fights microbes and helps regulate inflammation, with added roles in wound healing and immune signaling. 
Cartalax — A cartilage-targeted peptide bioregulator that’s promoted for joint and connective-tissue support, especially cartilage repair and inflammation control. 

Tanning/Cosmetic 
Melanotan I (Afamelanotide) — An FDA-approved tanning-related peptide that mainly boosts melanin to protect against UV damage in people with severe light sensitivity.
Melanotan II — A melanin-stimulating peptide known for producing darker skin tone and tanning effects, with stronger side-effect concerns.
PT-141 (Bremelanotide) — A sexual-health peptide that can boost arousal and libido, with the strongest results in that category.
GHK-Cu — A skin-repair peptide that supports collagen, wound healing, and a healthier-looking complexion.
Epitalon — An anti-aging peptide studied for improving cellular longevity signals and sleep-related rhythms, with limited human proof.
Matrixyl (Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4) — A cosmetic peptide aimed at smoothing fine lines by supporting collagen production and skin repair.
Argireline (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8) — A wrinkle-softening peptide that helps reduce expression lines by relaxing facial muscle contraction.
Snap-8 — A stronger cosmetic cousin of Argireline designed to target dynamic wrinkles and give a smoother skin appearance.

Sexual Health
PT-141 (Bremelanotide) — A sexual-health peptide that reliably boosts arousal and libido, with the strongest clinical results in that category.
Kisspeptin-10 — A reproductive signaling peptide that can stimulate the hormonal cascade upstream of fertility and testosterone production.
Kisspeptin-54 — A longer-acting kisspeptin form that drives the same reproductive signaling pathway with stronger duration.
Oxytocin — A bonding and social-signaling peptide that may support intimacy, stress reduction, and sexual response.
Melanotan II — A melanocortin peptide that can increase skin darkening and sometimes sexual desire, but with more side-effect concerns.
HCG* — A hormone-like peptide that supports testosterone production and fertility signaling, especially in men.
Gonadorelin* — A GnRH peptide used to trigger LH and FSH release, making it useful for fertility and hormonal stimulation.
Tadalafil — A long-acting PDE5 inhibitor that boosts nitric-oxide–mediated vasodilation, improving blood flow and erectile function.ncbi.nlm.nih+1
Aminotadalafil — A tadalafil analog discussed as a vasodilation and sexual-health compound, but with much less human data, so its results are more speculative than tadalafil’s.
HMG -A fertility hormone used to stimulate egg production in women and sperm production in men.

SOMA CHEMS: 

5-Amino-1MQ — No solid human nasal data, so the expected benefit is mostly speculative and unproven.
AOD-9604 — Nasal use is unlikely to add much; overall results for fat loss are weak even before route is considered.
BPC-157 — One of the more plausible nasal options for recovery support, but real human nasal efficacy data is still very limited.
Epithalon — Nasal delivery is not well validated, and any anti-aging effect remains largely theoretical.
Glutathione — Nasal delivery is mainly used for systemic antioxidant support, but results are inconsistent and not robustly proven.
MT-2 (Melanotan II) — Nasal use can work for tanning and libido effects, though tolerability and dosing consistency can be issues.
N-acetyl Selank — One of the better candidates for nasal delivery, with the best practical fit for calm focus and anxiety reduction.
N-acetyl Semax — Also a strong nasal candidate, with the best expected payoff for focus, alertness, and cognitive lift.
NAD+ — Nasal use is not especially strong or well proven compared with other routes.
Noopept — Nasal delivery may produce a faster onset, but overall efficacy remains modest and evidence is limited.
Oxytocin — Nasal delivery is the most established route for central/social effects, but real-world results are mixed.
PT-141 — Nasal use is plausible for sexual-response effects, but injectable bremelanotide has the stronger clinical track record.
TB-500 — Nasal efficacy is weakly supported at best, with most recovery claims relying on non-nasal and preclinical evidence.

Hair loss
GHK-Cu — A copper peptide that supports follicle growth, scalp repair, and anti-inflammatory signaling, making it one of the more promising hair-loss peptides.
PTD-DBM — A Wnt-pathway peptide that aims to reactivate dormant follicles and push hair back into the growth phase.
KPV — An anti-inflammatory peptide that may help hair loss mainly by calming scalp inflammation rather than directly driving regrowth.
RU58841 — A topical anti-androgen that blocks DHT at the scalp to slow miniaturization and preserve hair.
KX-826 — A scalp DHT-blocking compound designed to reduce androgen-driven shedding with minimal systemic exposure.
CB-03-01 — A local anti-androgen that works by reducing DHT effects at the follicle, helping slow pattern hair loss.
Topilutamide — A topical androgen blocker that helps protect follicles from DHT-driven thinning and shedding.

Growth Hormone Secretagogues:

Sermorelin — A GHRH analog that tells the pituitary to release more of your own growth hormone.
Tesamorelin — A stronger GHRH analog that boosts GH release and is known for reducing visceral fat.
CJC-1295 with DAC — A long-acting GHRH analog that keeps GH signaling elevated for longer.
Mod GRF 1-29 — A short-acting GHRH analog that gives a quick pulse of GH release.
Ipamorelin — A ghrelin-receptor agonist that triggers GH release with relatively selective signaling.
GHRP-2 — A potent ghrelin-like peptide that strongly stimulates GH release.
GHRP-6 — A GH secretagogue that boosts GH and also tends to increase hunger.
Hexarelin — A very strong GH secretagogue that drives a sharp GH pulse.
MK-677 / Ibutamoren — An oral ghrelin mimetic that increases GH and IGF-1 over time.
Anamorelin — A ghrelin receptor agonist that increases GH and appetite, mainly studied for wasting.
Macimorelin — A diagnostic ghrelin agonist used to test GH deficiency.
Ghrelin — The body’s natural hunger hormone that also stimulates GH release.
Acetamoren/mk777 — A non-peptide ghrelin-receptor agonist studied as a growth-hormone secretagogue that may raise GH and IGF-1 while supporting appetite, sleep, and lean-mass goals. 

Sleep 

DSIP — A sleep-support peptide studied for improving deep sleep, reducing stress, and helping the body relax. 
SR9009 — A circadian-modulating compound that may shift sleep timing later and reduce REM sleep in preclinical studies.
SR9011 — A circadian-modulating compound that may shift sleep timing later, with sleep effects less clearly defined than SR9009.
